## Full description (max 4000 chars)
```
Cadence AI Music generates instrumental music that adapts to you, in real time.

Your body is constantly telling a story — your heart rate rises on a run, your breathing slows as you drift toward sleep, your steps quicken on a morning commute. Cadence reads that story through Android Health Connect and your phone's sensors, and composes music that fits the moment you're in.

Grounded in the *iso-principle* — a method used in music therapy to match a listener's state before gently nudging it — Cadence doesn't just play a pre-made playlist. Every track is generated fresh based on what your body is doing right now.

FEATURES

• Real-time biometric reading — heart rate, HRV, sleep quality, SpO₂, steps, GPS speed, and current weather
• AI-generated instrumental music — no pre-recorded catalog; every song is composed live
• Scene detection — automatically adapts to running, walking, commuting, resting, focus, workout, and party modes
• Transparent reasoning — see exactly *why* a piece of music was generated: the biometric inputs, the estimated mental state (arousal, valence, stress, energy, focus), and the chosen style
• Manual adjustments — override the scene, pick a genre, set an energy bias, or type a free-form mood hint
• Taste profile — rate tracks and the app learns what you like over time
• Seamless playback — pre-buffered generation keeps music flowing without gaps
• Privacy-first — all biometric data is processed on-device; only anonymised summaries are sent for music generation

HOW IT WORKS

1. Cadence reads anonymised biometric and environmental context from Health Connect and your device sensors.
2. A language model translates that context into an estimated mental state and a matching musical style.
3. An AI music model generates a fresh instrumental track in that style.
4. Playback begins; when your state shifts meaningfully, a new track is queued.

No account is required. No personal data leaves your device. No ads.

REQUIREMENTS

• Android 10 or later (API 30+)
• Health Connect installed and permissions granted
• Location permission (for GPS speed and weather)
• A wearable that reports to Health Connect is recommended (Fitbit, Samsung Galaxy Watch, Pixel Watch, and any Health Connect–compatible device)

RESEARCH & TRANSPARENCY

Cadence AI Music is a functional prototype exploring biometric-adaptive music for everyday mood regulation and wellbeing. The full reasoning chain is exposed in the app so you can understand — and override — every decision the AI makes.
```
